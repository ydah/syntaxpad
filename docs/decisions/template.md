# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context and rationale

Describe the problem, constraints, and alternatives. Link primary sources and current specifications
instead of copying changing facts.

## Decision

State the chosen direction and its scope.

## Consequences

Record positive and negative consequences, operational obligations, and compatibility effects.

## Evidence

When citing a measurement, include its date, measured commit, and target, then link the matching
entry in `quality/records.md`. State when no measured evidence applies.

## Reconsider when

List observable conditions that should trigger a new decision. A superseding decision receives a new
ADR number; do not rewrite an accepted decision or rationale.
